# Names and logos

"PurgeCAD", "Syntelab" and "Syntelab Apps", and their logos and icons, identify the official app made by Syntelab.

**You may:**
- say that your project is "based on PurgeCAD by Syntelab" (this is required, see `ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md`);
- link to https://syntelab.com/apps/purge-cad;
- use the names to refer to the official app in articles, reviews and tutorials.

**You may not:**
- distribute a modified version under the name "PurgeCAD" or with its icon;
- suggest that your version is made, endorsed or supported by Syntelab.

Questions: see https://syntelab.com.
